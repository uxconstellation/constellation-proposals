The number, and why it is what it is

Phase one is R30,000, split half on start and half on launch. That is a founding-build rate. We are proving a faster build style on this project and you are getting the benefit of it, in exchange for a brand we want in our portfolio and the freedom to move quickly. Fabric Atelier, and anything that comes after it, prices at our standard rate.

What is not in the number: paid advertising, a copywriting overhaul of every product description beyond what your packaging already says, and photography. If you want any of those, they are on the next page rather than folded in quietly here.

Why there are so few numbers on this page

It would be easy to split this into a support tier, a content retainer, an hours pack and a strategy fee. It would add up to more, and it would mean charging you twice for the same hours: once as time and once as output. It also turns a working relationship into a meter. So there is one build price, one monthly choice, and one optional extra. Anything outside that we quote once, before we make it, at R850 an hour.

On the R16,000 itself: it sits at the top of the boutique band above and below any agency with an account manager in the middle, and the brand work and the content are the same job, done by the same person. What it does not include is paid media or community management. Nobody is running your ads or answering your comments, and we would rather say that plainly than fold it in and do it thinly.

03 · The Founding Partner Option

Or we take less,
and back the outcome.

There is a version of this where we stop being a cost line. We drop every monthly rate by 30 percent and take an agreed share of your online revenue instead. When the store is quiet you pay less than you would have. When it works we do well because you did. It only makes sense for us if the thing actually sells, which is exactly the point of offering it.

What we would still have to agree, in writing, before either of us signs anything

The share itself and what it applies to. Our starting position is online revenue only, after shipping and refunds, and nothing on wholesale or retail. How long it runs, and what happens at the end of it. How either of us walks away, and what the rate reverts to. None of that is settled by this page and none of it should be settled in a conversation nobody wrote down.

To be clear about the shape of it. We are not asking for equity, a seat on anything, or a say in what you make, and the build fee stays outside the arrangement entirely. This is a commercial agreement on the monthly work only. If you want a specific number rather than a principle, say so and we will put one in front of you this week.

04 · How We Work

Nothing ordinary
about the way we work either.

Four things you can hold us to, and a list of what we still need from you before day one.

01

You see it on day three

A homepage and a full product page, designed, before the rest is built. If the direction is wrong it is wrong on day three, when changing it costs a conversation instead of a rebuild.

02

Scope in writing before money moves

Whatever you tick on this page becomes an exact written scope. Nobody pays anything until you have read it and agreed that it says what you thought it said.

03

We flag rather than fill

If an asset is missing or a claim cannot be substantiated, you get told. We will not quietly substitute stock imagery, invent a product description, or strengthen a claim your packaging does not make.

04

You are not locked in

The store is yours, on your Shopify account, in your name, from day one. Care plans run monthly after a three month minimum. If you stop, you keep everything and nothing breaks.

What we need from you

The source artwork for the packs, so we can settle the two things we could not read. A product list with sizes, prices and stock. Your ingredient and compliance copy as it must legally appear. And your Shopify account, or five minutes with us to open one in your name.

Open for discussion

Every number and every boundary on this page is a starting position rather than a condition. If the phasing is wrong, if you want Fabric Atelier in from day one, if the care tiers do not match the way you actually work, say it and we will reprice it honestly instead of absorbing it quietly and resenting it in month four.

Two things we will not move on, and you should know them now. We will not use stock imagery for a brand that has already shot its own, and we will not ship something that fails accessibility. Everything else is a conversation.
