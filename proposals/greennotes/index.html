01 · What We Found

Your product is ready.
Your pictures are not.

Before writing a word of this we captured greennotes.co.za and four of your competitors, pulled your real assets, and measured your brand off your own pixels rather than asking you for a brand book. Everything below is a measurement, not an impression. Where we could not measure something, we say so.

Measured

You are running three different greens

Your logo mark reads #4AC5B1. Your primary button reads #00A884. Your text links read #006C53. Three greens, no single brand green. On social, where a post is judged in half a second against a competitor's post, that inconsistency is the difference between a brand and a company that has a logo.

Measured

White on your green fails accessibility

The white "Start now" label on your green button measures 3.03 to 1. WCAG 2.2 AA needs 4.5 to 1 for text that size. Dark text on the same green measures 6.93 to 1 and passes comfortably. This matters more on social than on the site, because a failing caption on a feed post is unreadable on a phone in daylight.

Measured

Every competitor moves. You do not.

We ran motion detection across the captures. Abridge runs GSAP and Lottie. Nabla runs GSAP. Heidi and WHOOP run Motion. Oura runs Motion and Barba. greennotes.co.za runs no motion library at all, and neither does Healthbridge. The whole local category is standing still, which is exactly why moving is worth so much right now.

Measured

You have nine real product assets already

Your site carries nine product images: the recorder, the SOAP note, the letter on a phone, the letter as a PDF, the body map, the growth chart, the calendar, the intake form and the scanner. These are the best raw material you have and they are being used as small supporting thumbnails. They should be the heroes.

The one thing we could not measure

Your LinkedIn posts and your YouTube uploads sit behind a sign in wall we did not push past, so we have not seen the creative you are running today. The follower count is public and verified. The creative is not. Before we finalise anything we want you to send us the last twenty posts, or add us as a page viewer, so the audit is complete rather than half done. We would rather flag the gap than fill it with a guess.

What the category is actually doing

We captured Heidi, Abridge, Nabla, Oura and WHOOP. In the AI scribe category the pattern is unanimous and it is the same pattern every time: a photograph of a human being, full bleed, with type laid over it. Abridge shows a smiling patient. Heidi shows a real consulting room. Both are good. Both are also completely interchangeable, and there is no reason a South African clinician would recognise either as speaking to them.

You are the only company in this set whose homepage leads with the product instead of a person. You did that by instinct. It happens to be the Apple move, and it is the opening we want to take.

02 · What "The Apple Of Medical" Actually Means

It is not white space.
It is nerve.

Every brand that says it wants to look like Apple means clean type on a white field, and every brand that stops there ends up looking like a template. We pulled the actual Apple product page apart to find the rules underneath. There are five, and four of them are about restraint under pressure rather than taste.

The ruleWhat Apple actually doesWhat it means for GreenNotes
One object, enormous The AirPods Pro occupy over half the frame, floating on a near white field, lit like a still life. Nothing else is in the picture. One artefact per post. A single SOAP note. A single completed W.Cl.4. Not a feature grid, not three phones fanned out.
Type sits under the object, never on it Eyebrow, then one bold claim, then one line of fine print. The product is never obscured by its own headline. Kills the legibility problem before it starts, and it is why we will never put a drop shadow behind a caption.
Exactly one accent The entire page is greyscale except one blue Buy button. The accent means "act", nowhere else. One GreenNotes green, used only where a clinician is meant to do something. This is the discipline your three greens currently lack.
The claim is specific and falsifiable "Up to 2x more than AirPods Pro 2." Not "incredible sound". A number someone could check. "Two hours of consultation. Fourteen seconds of typing." Your product is full of these. Vague benefit copy is the enemy.
Silence is the effect No decoration, no gradient mesh, no floating icons. The confidence is in what has been left out. Your own word for this is already "quietly". The brand line and the design law are the same sentence.

Where we deviate from Apple, deliberately

Apple sells an object you can hold. You sell an absence: the admin that did not happen, the evening that did not get eaten. So we borrow Apple's grammar and change the noun. The hero object is not a device. It is the finished artefact your software produced while the clinician was talking to a patient.

Oura does the same trick and it is worth naming as the second reference, because they photograph a ring on a lava rock with a ladybird on it and make a health product feel like a natural object rather than a gadget. That warmth is what stops this direction going cold, which is the real risk with a medical brand in a near white palette.

03 · The Direction

Quietly handled.
Show the artefact.

We are not proposing a new line. Yours is better than anything we would write for a pitch. We are proposing that "quietly handled" stops being a headline and becomes the law that governs every post: each piece of creative shows one thing that got handled, and says almost nothing about it.

Your competitors put a doctor's face on the screen. We put the note on the screen. The note is the proof, the note is the product, and the note is the only thing in the category nobody else can show, because nobody else's note is theirs. The rule on generative AI, in writing

No generative AI appears as content in anything we deliver. No AI generated people, clinics, hands, faces, product shots or scenes. Every image is your real product, your real people or a licensed photograph, and every asset ships with a note saying which. If a moment genuinely calls for it, we will bring it to you first and it does not get made until you say yes in writing.

This is not only a taste position. You sell an AI product into a profession that is nervous about AI. A clinician who spots a synthetic face in your feed will wonder what else is synthetic, and that is a cost you should never have to pay for a stock image.

04 · What We Make, Every Month

Build the system once.
Then it is fast.

The direction sprint is not a moodboard for its own sake. It ends with a built template system, so that from month two a new post is an hour of art direction rather than a day of design. That is what makes the retainer sustainable at this price, and it is the part most studios leave out.

Static

Twelve posts a month

Across 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16. Artefact heroes, proof posts, clinician quotes, feature launches and the guides you already publish, cut for feed.

Motion

Three moving pieces a month

Six to fifteen seconds. The assembling artefact, product walkthroughs cut from real screen capture, and launch teasers. Delivered as MP4 plus the source.

Direction

Ongoing art direction

A monthly plan against what you are actually shipping, a review call, and the standing right to phone us when something changes.

Formats

One design, every size

Each concept is built once and resolved into every placement, so a LinkedIn post, a Story and an in app announcement stay the same idea rather than three near misses.

Copy

Captions written, not filled in

Written in the voice we agree in the sprint. Short, specific, no exclamation marks, no emoji ladders, nothing that sounds like a SaaS newsletter.

Compliance

Nothing clinical goes out unchecked

Any asset showing patient facing data uses fabricated demonstration records, never a real one, and anything making a clinical claim goes to you before it goes anywhere.

What makes this a partnership rather than an invoice. The rate is held for twelve months while you grow, so scaling from a handful of clinicians to a few hundred does not trigger a repricing conversation. In exchange we ask for three things that cost you nothing: the right to show the work as a case study once it is live, an introduction when a clinician asks who does your creative, and honest feedback fast enough that we can act on it.

All of this is open for discussion

None of these numbers are a position we are defending. They are a starting point built from what the work actually takes, put in writing so you have something specific to push against rather than a range.

So push against it. If the monthly is more than you want to commit to before you have seen us work, take the direction sprint on its own and decide afterwards, with six finished assets in front of you. The one thing we will not do is quietly thin the work to hit a number. If the budget moves, the scope moves with it and we will tell you exactly what changed.

06 · How We Work Together

Confirm, do not ask.
Then hold the line.

You are running a company with eight people in it and you do not have time to answer a questionnaire every week. So we work the other way around: we make the call, show you the call, and you correct it. You will never get an email from us asking what you think the brand should feel like.

Direction before campaigns, always

Nothing runs as a campaign until the moodboard and direction are signed off. This is your condition and we have built the whole engagement around it rather than treating it as a preference.

No generative AI as content

Written into the scope, not left as an understanding. If a case ever comes up where it genuinely serves the work, we bring it to you first and it stays unmade until you agree in writing.

We decide, you correct

Every deliverable arrives as a decision with the reasoning attached, not a set of options to choose between. Two rounds of revision are included on each monthly batch.

Measured, never asserted

Any claim we make about your brand, your creative or an asset's accessibility is computed from the actual pixels and we will show you the working. Nothing in this document was estimated by eye.

Your assets stay yours

Every source file, template and export transfers to GreenNotes. If we ever stop working together you keep the system and can hand it to anyone.

One person, no layers

Aldo runs this directly. There is no account manager between you and the work, and no brief gets relayed through someone who was not in the room.

What we need from you, and it is short

Confirm rather than answer. One: your last twenty social posts, or page access, so the audit is complete. Two: your real logo files and any brand book that exists, so we resolve your green rather than replace it. Three: a name on your side who can approve a clinical claim inside a day. Four: whether the Billing and Claims launch date is still August.

If any of those is a problem, none of it blocks the direction sprint. We start with what we have and flag what is missing in writing rather than working around it silently.
