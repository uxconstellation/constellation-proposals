01 — The Opportunity

A smart engine
needs a road to drive on.

My Mobile has the infrastructure competitors are still trying to build: a fully agentic marketing platform, a financing product purpose-built for the SA market, and a growing B2C Shopify presence. What is missing is the layer between the engine room and the customer: consistent content, a recognisable brand voice, and an education strategy that explains why contract beats prepaid and how Whatto makes it possible.

This engagement covers three areas. Marketing activation: deploy the platform, train the brand identity, build a content engine that runs. Website and Shopify: a reusable management model so the team owns their store without developer dependency. AI adoption: consolidate four tools into one governed stack, build role-specific skills, and route all outputs through a single validation layer before anything goes to market.

Marketing inconsistency

No regular posting cadence, no content strategy in market. The platform is built; the output is not yet flowing. Consumers cannot buy from a brand they do not see.

Brand recognition gap

Consumers know iStore. My Mobile is the brand doing more for the South African market, but that story is not being told at scale or with enough consistency to land.

EVP and Whatto education

The blue-collar audience needs a clear reason to choose contract over prepaid, and a plain-language explanation of how Whatto financing works. No current content addresses this directly.

Platform not yet deployed

Keshav's 11-agent dashboard is locally hosted. It needs a live environment, CI training, and a workflow before the team can use it reliably. The asset exists; it is not activated.

Website and Shopify not conversion-ready

The B2C Shopify store is live but not optimised for the EVP audience or campaign traffic. Store updates require developer involvement. Every delay in updating banners, products, or promotions is lost revenue.

Fragmented AI stack with no governance

Four AI tools (Copilot, Manus, Claude, ChatGPT) running in parallel across the team with no clear ownership, no security policy, and no single source of truth. Outputs cannot be validated consistently and data governance is unclear.

04 — AI Adoption & Governance

One stack.
One source of truth.
Every output validated.

Running four AI tools in parallel is not a strategy — it is a liability. Different people using different tools for the same tasks means inconsistent outputs, unclear data governance, and skills that are siloed by personal preference rather than built for the business. This engagement replaces that with a governed, purposeful stack where every tool has a clear function, every person has a skill set, and every output passes through a single validation layer before it reaches the market.

The 11-agent platform becomes the single agentic model: the orchestration layer that produces, validates, and approves content before distribution. Not a review step — a structural guarantee.

AI stack audit and tool mapping

Map current usage of Copilot, Manus, Claude, and ChatGPT across the team. Identify where tools overlap, where they conflict, and where data governance is unclear. Output: a clean function-to-tool assignment that removes duplication and gives each person one primary tool for each task type.

Unified toolset configuration

Configure the agreed stack per role. Copilot for Office and financial automation (Nischan). Manus for research and planning (Satish). Claude as the content, validation, and analysis layer (all marketing outputs). ChatGPT retired from the marketing workflow — redundant given the above. Each tool scoped and governed, not just recommended.

Security and compliance framework

Define what data is permitted in each tool. No customer PII in public AI endpoints. API key management centralised. Clear policy for what constitutes a validated vs. draft output. Reduces security exposure from a team using AI tools ad hoc, and provides a defensible compliance position as AI regulation in SA matures.

Single agentic source of truth

The 11-agent platform is configured as the master output layer. All marketing content — social posts, campaign copy, Whatto explainers, tips and tricks — is generated, reviewed, and approved through the platform before distribution. One place to audit, one place to override, one place to measure quality. No rogue outputs from personal ChatGPT sessions.

Role-specific playbooks and skill-building

Each team member (Satish, Nischan, Keshav, Arianna) receives a written playbook: which tool to use for which task, how to prompt effectively, what outputs to review before sending, and how to escalate when the AI gets it wrong. Built to the level of the person, not a generic AI training course.

Ongoing governance as the team grows

As My Mobile brings on new staff or expands AI use into new functions, the governance framework scales with them. Playbooks are living documents, updated as tools evolve. Constellation reviews the stack quarterly and recommends changes as new capabilities become available.
