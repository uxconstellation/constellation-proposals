01 — The Opportunity

A smart engine
needs a road to drive on.

My Mobile has the infrastructure competitors are still trying to build: a fully agentic marketing platform, a financing product purpose-built for the SA market, and a growing B2C Shopify presence. What is missing is the layer between the engine room and the customer: consistent content, a recognisable brand voice, and an education strategy that explains why contract beats prepaid and how Whatto makes it possible.

This engagement has two stages. First, a clear-eyed assessment of what exists and what gaps remain. Then, an activation layer: deploying the platform, training the brand identity into the AI agents, and building a content engine that runs.

Marketing inconsistency

No regular posting cadence, no content strategy in market. The platform is built; the output is not yet flowing. Consumers cannot buy from a brand they do not see.

Brand recognition gap

Consumers know iStore. My Mobile is the brand doing more for the South African market, but that story is not being told at scale or with enough consistency to land.

EVP and Whatto education

The blue-collar audience needs a clear reason to choose contract over prepaid, and a plain-language explanation of how Whatto financing works. No current content addresses this directly.

Platform not yet deployed

Keshav's 11-agent dashboard is locally hosted. It needs a live environment, CI training, and a workflow before the team can use it reliably. The asset exists; it is not activated.

05 — How We Work Together

Clear process.
No surprises.

All Constellation engagements are governed by a signed Statement of Work and an NDA before any work begins. The working model is built around your team's existing tools and pace, not ours.

NDA and Statement of Work

Both parties sign an NDA and formal SOW before work begins. The SOW confirms scope, deliverables, timeline, revision policy, and acceptance criteria. No scope changes proceed without a written amendment and approval.

Phase-gated delivery

Phase 1 (assessment) completes with your sign-off on the recommendations report before Phase 2 (activation) begins. Nothing advances without a clear approval milestone. Transparent progress throughout.

Working with your AI stack

Constellation works alongside the team's existing tools: Copilot (most used), Manus (Satish's preference), Claude, ChatGPT. We do not replace your stack; we make the marketing platform built on it actually produce output.

Communication and cadence

Async-first with weekly progress updates. Monthly strategy review with Satish. Direct access to Aldo for strategic decisions. Platform-level work coordinated directly with Keshav and Arianna as needed.

Hourly transparency

Retainer hours are logged and reported monthly. If a month runs under the committed hours, unused time rolls into a bank for burst work the following month. No hours are silently absorbed.

Intellectual property

All deliverables (strategy documents, content frameworks, platform configuration, training data) transfer to My Mobile upon full payment. Constellation retains the right to reference the engagement as a case study unless otherwise agreed in the SOW.
