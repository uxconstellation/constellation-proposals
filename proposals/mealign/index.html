01 · The Vision

Wellness meets
clinical precision.
All in one place.

Mealign sits at the intersection of prescription medicine and personal performance. Users consult with a licensed doctor, receive a tailored peptide prescription, and manage their entire health protocol: supplementation, dosage reminders, progress tracking, all within a single, trusted platform. It is a clinical wellness ecosystem designed to keep users aligned with their health goals long after the first prescription is filled.

The UK market has validated the model: Voy and Numan proved that a clean, credible digital experience can make prescription wellness mainstream. Mealign takes that further, with a richer member environment designed for retention, not just conversion. This engagement builds the foundation: the marketing surface that drives acquisition, and the web app that delivers the full clinical journey.

No digital presence yet

The brand and vision exist, but there is no platform to drive signups, explain the offer, or build trust with a health-conscious audience that has high expectations of digital credibility.

The clinical workflow has to be digital-first

Doctors need a portal that manages consultations, issues prescriptions, and handles patient records without manual friction. Getting this wrong creates compliance risk and a poor experience for both sides.

Prescriptions are sensitive data

Patient health records, prescription history, and payment details require a security-first architecture from day one. GDPR compliance is not optional; it is the baseline for operating in this space.

Subscription checkout needs to be frictionless

Charging at prescription approval, managing recurring subscriptions, and linking product delivery to a prescription record requires a payment layer that is both secure and seamless for the user.

Doctor time is the scarcest resource

Every unnecessary click in the doctor portal is time taken from patient care. The doctor-side UX needs to be fast, opinionated, and purpose-built for a clinical workflow, not a generic admin interface.

Retention is the real metric

Acquiring a member is the easy part. Keeping them engaged across nutrition, supplementation, dosage tracking, and ongoing care is what makes Mealign a platform, not just a prescription service.

02
Phase 2 · Weeks 2 to 4 · MVP Web Application

The clinical engine. Doctor portal to member dashboard.

Authentication: two portals, one platform. Separate login flows for members and doctors. Members access their personal health dashboard; doctors access their patient management interface. Role-based permissions built into the database from day one.

Booking and consultation: Members book a consultation slot from their dashboard. Doctors see their scheduled appointments. The consultation happens in-platform via embedded video with no app downloads or third-party redirects. One clean experience for both sides.

Prescription management: Doctors issue prescriptions directly from the consultation interface. The prescription is stored against the member's account, visible on their dashboard, and triggers the subscription checkout. Full history retained and accessible to both member and doctor.

Subscription billing: Stripe powers the payment layer. The subscription is activated at prescription approval, with no manual invoicing or payment delays. Recurring billing managed automatically. Members can view their billing history and manage their subscription from their dashboard.

Member dashboard, the retention layer: Active prescriptions and dosage schedule, medication reminders, progress tracking, product reorder. The dashboard is where members live between consultations: it keeps them engaged with their health protocol and connected to the platform.

Security and compliance: GDPR-compliant architecture throughout: encrypted patient data, role-based access control, no anonymous access to protected records. Built to UK health data standards from the first line of code, not retrofitted later.

04 · Technical Approach

Built for speed.
Secured for health data.

Mealign handles prescription data, payment records, and clinical notes. The architecture reflects that: a modern React frontend backed by Supabase for database, auth, and encrypted file storage, with Stripe for payments and an embedded video SDK for consultations. Every component is chosen for healthcare-adjacent trust requirements, not just developer convenience. The build is powered by Claude Code and Lovable working in tandem: Claude Code writes, reviews, and secures the codebase directly; Lovable accelerates the UI layer. Together they compress a 4-month agency timeline into 5 weeks without dropping quality or compliance standards.

Frontend

React + Next.js

Component-based, fast-loading, and straightforward to expand. The marketing site and web app share a codebase, keeping long-term maintenance clean.

Database + Auth

Supabase

PostgreSQL database with row-level security, built-in GDPR tooling, role separation between member and doctor access, and encrypted storage for clinical documents.

Payments

Stripe

Subscription management, prescription-triggered billing, recurring payments, and member payment history. PCI-compliant out of the box, no raw card data stored.

Video Consultations

Whereby Embedded

Browser-based video calls embedded directly into the platform. No app downloads for the member. GDPR-compliant, HIPAA-aware, simple to integrate and support.

Design Reference

Voy + Numan (UK)

The visual benchmark for the UK prescription wellness market. Slick, clinical, and credible. We build to this aesthetic using Mealign's purple and cream brand palette.

Compliance

GDPR (UK)

UK data protection standards applied from day one: patient data encrypted at rest, access logged, no anon access to protected records, clear data retention policies built in.

06 · How We Work Together

Clear process.
No surprises.

All Constellation engagements are governed by a signed Statement of Work before any build begins. The SOW locks the scope, payment milestones, revision policy, and delivery criteria for both phases. No scope changes proceed without a written amendment and approval from both parties.

NDA and Statement of Work

Both parties sign an NDA and a formal SOW before the first line of code is written. The SOW details the full Phase 1 and Phase 2 scope, payment milestones, deliverables, and revision policy. No ambiguity on either side.

Milestone-based delivery

Phase 1 and Phase 2 each have clear delivery milestones with sign-off points. You review what is built at each milestone before the next stage begins. No surprises at the end of a 5-week build.

Brand and design alignment

Before any UI work begins, we align on the visual direction: type, spacing, Mealign's purple and cream palette applied correctly, and the Voy and Numan benchmark translated into something distinctly Mealign. Design sign-off happens before build.

Communication and cadence

Async-first with weekly progress updates. Fortnightly check-in calls at key build stages. Direct access to Aldo for any design or technical decision that needs a quick turnaround. No account manager layers.

Revisions policy

Two revision rounds included per phase, covered within the project fee. Any revision that changes agreed scope is quoted and approved separately before work begins. The SOW defines what constitutes a scope change.

Handover and IP

All deliverables transfer to Mealign on final payment: the codebase, design assets, and any documentation. Constellation retains the right to reference the project as a case study unless otherwise agreed in the SOW.
