Euroseat · Launch Roadmap
Project Roadmap

Getting Euroseat live, in one focused push.

We are taking this over the line ourselves. This roadmap turns the full plan into a clear sequence: one working session together to agree and switch on the quick wins, then a focused build the moment we have access. Roughly five to seven working days from kickoff, with two days built in as buffer.

Estimated completion: Friday 3 July 2026
Go-live critical Included now (goodwill) Client / together
Stage 1
Day 1 · the session

Kickoff working session (you and Esmée)

Together · one sitting · ~90 min
  • Walk the master plan, confirm priorities and clear up the "status unknown" items
  • Agree the app list and that subscriptions sit on Euroseat's account
  • Switch on the quick wins live on the call goodwill: Google reviews, Shop the Look, back-in-stock, loyalty and affiliate foundations
  • Decide Twinfield vs SnelStart with the accountant, and the invoicing app
  • Hand over staff access so we can start, and confirm what Euroseat will deliver (costs, B2B list, content, payment connection)
Stage 2
1–2 days

Core build, on access

Constellation · direct configuration
  • Categories and the left-hand filters incl. the new dimension filter for tables go-live
  • Smart search: synonyms (girola/girolo) and redirects
  • B2B company structure, catalogues and pricing; private vs business rule
  • Tiered (staffel) pricing applied automatically on order, VAT incl/excl finished
  • Quote to invoice flow with the approve-and-pay step
  • Inventory display rules (21-day "Net binnen", 14-day sold-out) and product stock-card PDF finalised
Stage 3
1–2 days

Apps and integrations

Constellation · install & configure
  • Postcode + order-value shipping tiers go-live
  • Branded invoices, quotes and packing slips (chosen app, template to brand)
  • Accounting coupling (Twinfield or SnelStart) with the accountant
  • HubSpot sync, newsletter and abandoned cart, SEO, multilingual goodwill
  • Analytics (GA4) installed ahead of go-live
In parallel
alongside · 2–3 days

What Euroseat does alongside us

Client · the only things that gate launch
  • Connect the Mollie payment account (iDEAL, in3) and the accounting login client
  • Enter purchase prices using the template provided
  • Provide the B2B customer and pricing list
  • Finalise content: About Us, social links, any project entries
Stage 4
1 day

QA and go-live

Constellation · launch
  • Full run-through on staging, fix anything outstanding
  • DNS and final checks, then take the store live
  • Send the customer account-activation mail to migrated customers
Stage 5
2 days · buffer

Cleanups and polish

Constellation · settle-in week
  • Finish configuration on the goodwill apps (loyalty rules, translations, flows)
  • Tidy edge cases that only surface once the store is trading
  • This sits inside the complimentary one-month support window
Later
Scoped separately

Phase 2

Quoted as a second phase
  • Satellite portal, route planner, purchasing processing, rich choice-helper, personalisation, literal e-signature on quotes
  • We scope and price these once the store is live and trading
Managing expectations

The honest dependency

We have built two days of buffer into the plan, which is why the estimated completion is Friday 3 July. The timeline holds provided two things land on time: staff access granted at the kickoff session, and Euroseat completing its own items, connecting payments, entering costs, and supplying the B2B list and content. With those in hand, everything else is on us.
