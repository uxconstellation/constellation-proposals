01 · Opening

Thank you for letting us read your brief so closely. It is rare to receive one written with this much care, and it told us as much about the feeling you want as the pages you need.

So before we talk about structure or screens, we want you to know what we heard underneath it all. You are not asking for a better shop. You are asking for somewhere a person can arrive, slow down, and feel they have met you, your work, and the quiet space you make it in, before anyone thinks about owning a piece of it.

That is the website we would like to build with you.

02 · What we heard

Your work helps women feel seen. The site should do the same for anyone who finds it.

It should feel warm, honest and quietly luxurious. Minimal, but never cold. Rooted in craft. It should read like you talking, in the first person, gently, without selling or urgency or the language of empowerment that you are so right to avoid.

And it should resolve a quiet tension in your current site. Today, sarasian.com is built as a shop, with products, categories and a checkout at its centre. Your brief is the opposite instinct. You want a studio that happens to sell, not a store that happens to show art. Everything we propose flows from that single reframe.

03 · The idea

One organising principle holds the whole site together.

Stepping into a quiet studio, with the door gently closed behind you.

In practice that means three things. Space, so the work can breathe and the eye can rest. Slowness, so nothing rushes the visitor toward a decision. And restraint, so that every element earns its place and the artwork is always the loudest thing on the page.

This is not minimalism for its own sake. It is hospitality. We are designing the feeling of being welcomed into a room, shown a few things that matter, and trusted to stay as long as you like.

04 · The experience

Your own note said it best: fewer pages, more clarity. So rather than seven pages, we suggest five, each one doing more with less.

HomeThe welcome

An unhurried welcome. A single piece of artwork given room, a few words of philosophy, and gentle pathways onward. A "reflections from the studio" moment lower down, with two or three journal excerpts and beautiful imagery. Your Instagram woven in softly near the foot, as it is today.

My StoryThe person

You, your journey, and the gallery in its historic George home. This is where studio and gallery imagery lives, where credibility is felt rather than claimed, and where the visitor decides they have met a real person.

The WorkOriginals & prints

One considered space for originals and prints together, with the story and meaning beside each piece, not a price tag in front of it. Originals invite enquiry. Prints can be admired now and bought simply later, when you are ready for that.

WorkshopsThe invitation

The creative experiences you offer, with room for video from Instagram so the warmth carries through. A gentle way to be part of your world without owning a painting.

Reach OutThe conversation

Your contact page, reframed. Not a transaction, but an invitation to begin a conversation, book a studio visit, or simply say hello.

Navigation stays elegant and uncluttered throughout, carried by your softer language: an invitation to explore, allowing the work to unfold, reach out rather than contact, connect rather than buy.

06 · The work, and how it sells

Here we make the most important choice, and it is the one your brief is quietly asking for. We lead with enquiry, not checkout.

Each original is one of a kind and deserves a conversation, so originals invite the visitor to reach out, and that first message becomes the start of a relationship rather than a card payment. It protects the unhurried feeling, and it suits how you already work, where so much begins on social media and in your gallery.

Prints can follow the same gentle path at launch, presented beautifully with their story, materials and quality, available by enquiry. When you are ready, we can add a simple, calm checkout for prints as a later step, without ever turning the site back into a shop.

A note on the currency converter. Live converters tend to add clutter and the occasional glitch, which works against the quiet we are building. We suggest pricing in your own currency with a soft "approximately" line for international visitors. It is calmer, more reliable, and entirely in keeping. If real international selling grows, proper multi-currency can be added later.

07 · How we build it

We build on Lovable, which lets us hand-craft something that feels bespoke, quickly and affordably.

For you that means a premium, made-for-you site without a bespoke-build budget, and a fast path from approval to something real you can see and feel.

One honest point about keeping it current. Your journal and reflections are part of what makes the site live, and we want them to stay easy to add to. At this budget we suggest the simplest, most reliable arrangement: you write your reflections in your own time, and we publish them for you as part of a small monthly studio-care arrangement that also keeps everything updated, backed up and running well. If you would rather post entirely on your own, we can build in a light content system as an optional upgrade. Either way, you are never stuck.
