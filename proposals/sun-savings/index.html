01 · What We Found

The offer is fine.
The site is the problem.

Before writing this we went through sunsavings.co.za properly: every URL in your sitemap, the load times, the page weights, the prices on each page. Not a glance at the homepage. Everything below is measured, and you can check any of it yourself in a browser right now.

Sun Savings sells solar from R799 a month with no upfront cost. In a country that has lived through load-shedding, that is an easy thing to want. The site is not losing you people because the offer is weak. It is losing them because it cannot get out of its own way.

Thirty-six demo product pages

Four duplicate shops, each holding the same nine Squarespace template products. Golden Mist Pair, Earth Sky Planter, Milk Dip Cup. All live, all indexable, all priced R50, all described in lorem ipsum.

Three homepages, three FAQs

Home, home-1 and home-2 all load. So do three contact pages, three FAQ pages, two pricing pages and two products pages. Google has to guess which one matters, and it guesses wrong.

Three different prices

The page title says from R1750 a month. The headline says R1599.99. The package cards say R1 390 on one page and R2 478 on another. A visitor comparing quotes cannot tell you what you charge.

Two of everything else

Two phone numbers and two email addresses are in circulation across the site and your search listings. Every one of those is a call that might not reach you.

Heavy where it hurts

A decorative background image weighs 906KB and loads twice. Partner logos run to 108KB each as PNGs when they should be 5KB. The page is 625KB before a single photo. On a phone on mobile data, that is where people leave.

A typo in the URL structure

All ten partner brand pages, Sunsynk, Deye, Hubble, JA Solar and the rest, sit under a folder called testomonial-1. Every one of those links carries the misspelling.

The one thing worth protecting

Fifteen blog articles on rent-to-own, solar financing and apartment solar. That is real, on-topic content earning real search traffic, and it is the most valuable thing on the site. Every one of them moves across with its URL intact, which is exactly why the rebuild needs a proper redirect map and not a fresh start.
