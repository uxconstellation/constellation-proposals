01 · The Opportunity

One inbox today.
One platform
tomorrow.

Everything LabTQA does already runs through one shared inbox. The CRM is the urgent piece, and your 28-page specification is roughly 80% there. What you are really building is an operational hub, with the CRM as the first and most important piece of it.

You want a system you operate, where AI surfaces answers like "what is the status of this client?" but never takes silent action on a record. You want it delivered in your priority order, and because it is the medical field, you want traceability and compliance designed in from the first line of code, not retrofitted later.

The shared inbox has no spine

Mail lands in one mailbox with no reference numbers, no clear ownership, and no memory of who followed up or when. The daily work has nothing holding it together.

Renewals season is manual

Identifying who is due, quoting from last year, and attaching the right document to the right lab is done by hand, against the clock, every year.

Traceability is the law

The medical field means nothing can be quietly lost and every change must be provable. POPIA and ISO 13485 are the floor, not a feature you add later.

Complaints must be provable

Complaint-related emails need to be recognised, classified, and kept auditable, with corrective actions linked to the case throughout its life.

Campaigns go to many labs at once

Renewal and results campaigns send to grouped lists with per-recipient documents. One release should create one tracked case per recipient, not a manual mess.

It has to be one system, not five

Quality, shipping and HR live in separate tools and spreadsheets today. The value is one platform they all run on, built in your priority order.

04 · Technical Approach & Security

Built for speed.
Secured for medical data.

LabTQA handles contact PII, email correspondence, complaints, and financial records, and your IT team is right to approve any solution before it goes live, so this section is written for them. The custom options run on a modern React frontend backed by Supabase, a managed PostgreSQL, with row-level security, an append-only audit trail, and a Vault for sensitive identifiers, hosted in a region and cloud you approve. Your data is never fed to public AI models. Where AI surfaces information it does so inside the app, on your own data, under your access controls, and the tools you already run, ChatGPT, Claude Pro and Copilot, stay under your existing governance.

For your IT team

IT signs off before you commit

You raised the real risk of agreeing on an option your IT team later rejects. We remove it. Before any commitment, we hand your IT a written security and data-architecture overview for the option you favour, covering where data lives, access control, encryption, audit, and how it fits your POPIA and internal policies. You commit only once your IT is comfortable.

Your data, and the AI tools you already use

Introducing a new tool should not mean new data exposure. Your data lives in a dedicated, encrypted instance in a region you approve, and it is never sent to public AI models. Every option plugs into your existing Microsoft 365 and works with Copilot rather than replacing it, and Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude Pro stay under your existing governance; we do not route your records through them. Access is role-based, so adding a staff member is a permission you grant, not a new data risk.

On enhancing Outlook and Copilot

You asked whether Outlook plus plugins and Copilot could achieve this. We have built that thinking in: every option plugs into Outlook and works with Copilot. On its own, though, Outlook and plugins cannot give you reference-numbered cases, renewal tracking, an audit trail or QMS records, so we use Outlook and Copilot as part of the ecosystem rather than forcing the structure into them.

HIPAA: confirmed out of scope

We confirmed with you that HIPAA is not a requirement here. LabTQA handles laboratory and QA metadata and B2B contact data, not identifiable patient health records. We still apply HIPAA-aligned safeguards, encryption, access logging and audit, as a quality bar, but HIPAA is not a compliance obligation we design against; POPIA and ISO 13485 remain the frameworks that govern this build.

06 · How We Work Together

Clear process.
No surprises.

The speed comes from being AI-first. The trust comes from how we hold the line: a signed Statement of Work before any build, proof over assertion, and a person in the loop wherever judgement matters.

NDA, IT sign-off, then Statement of Work

We work under your NDA, or provide a mutual one. Your IT then reviews and signs off the security architecture for your chosen option before you commit. A Statement of Work locks scope, milestones and revision policy before any code is written.

Phased, milestone-based delivery

You commit one phase at a time, each with a clear delivery milestone and a sign-off point. You review what is built before the next phase begins. No surprises at the end.

Two-round QA

Functional testing against the spec, then pressure testing at load and at the edges, before any phase is signed off. We verify with proof, we do not assert.

Application-driven, human in the loop

You operate the system. AI surfaces answers, it never takes silent action on a client record, and steps like a Sage sales order or a quote match are confirmed by a person by design.

Communication and cadence

Async-first with weekly progress updates and check-in calls at key build stages. Direct access to Aldo for any decision that needs a quick turnaround. No account-manager layers.

Ownership and protection

Under Option A you own the code and data outright on final payment. Under Option B your data is always yours and exportable, the platform is never licensed to a direct competitor of yours, and we hold the source code in escrow so you are never left stranded.
