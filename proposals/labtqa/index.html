01 · What We Heard

You are not buying a CRM. You are building an operational hub.

Everything the lab does already runs through one shared inbox. The CRM is the urgent piece, and your specification is roughly 80% there. The rest sharpens the moment you can see and use a first version, which is exactly how we like to work: ship something real early, then let reality shape the last stretch.

Reading your 28-page specification and the notes from our two calls, four things came through clearly. This proposal is built around them.

The shared-inbox problem

Everything lands in one mailbox with no reference numbers, no clear ownership, and no memory of who followed up or when. The daily work has no spine.

Renewals under pressure

Renewal season is manual: identifying who is due, quoting from last year, and attaching the right document to the right lab, all by hand and against the clock.

Traceability is the law

Medical field means nothing can be quietly lost and every change must be provable. POPIA and ISO 13485 are the floor, not a feature.

Application-driven, not agent-driven

You want a system you operate, where AI surfaces answers like "what is the status of this client?" but never takes silent action on a record.

Protected, and mapped per phase.

One item to confirm on HIPAA

HIPAA applies directly only if the system stores identifiable patient health information. Our reading is that LabTQA handles laboratory and QA metadata and B2B contact data, not patient records, so we design to HIPAA-aligned safeguards (encryption, access control, audit, minimum-necessary) as a quality bar, and confirm actual scope with you at kickoff.
