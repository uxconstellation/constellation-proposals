01 · What each fee is for

Three fees.
No surprises.

One build fee for the engine and the first game, one small fee each time it reskins to a new brand, and a monthly to keep the live campaign running. That is the whole picture.

One-off

Build · R95,000

The reusable game engine, the full Chikka Chicken game, the proof-of-purchase (till-slip) flow, the live leaderboard, and a light admin to run a campaign. Built once, owned by SPAR, reused by every brand after.

Per brand

Reskin · R28,000

Each new house brand: new art, palette, characters and copy dropped onto the same engine, with QA and redeploy. No game logic rebuilt. Billed to that brand's own department.

Monthly

Hosting · R4,000

Hosting, the leaderboard backend, running the live campaign, monitoring, and same-day fixes. Only one game is live at a time, which keeps this lean.

Domain and third-party subscriptions (hosting, database) are passed through at cost, from about R350 a year for a domain. All amounts in ZAR, exclusive of VAT.

02 · Why a reskin is a fraction of the build

Build once.
Reskin cheap.

The expensive part, the engine, the scoring, the leaderboard, the proof-of-purchase, the admin, is built one time and never rebuilt. A reskin only changes what a shopper sees: the brand art, colours, characters and copy. That is why the second brand, and the tenth, cost a fraction of the first.

03 · How we address the things that matter

The concerns, and what we build for each.

Fair play, no cheating

Anyone plays, and only a winner uploads a slip to redeem. That slip is single-use and checked server-side, and bot and rate limiting stop gaming the leaderboard. No card or banking data is ever stored.

Your customers' data (POPIA)

No personal data is needed to play. Contact details are collected only when someone wins, with consent and opt-out, encrypted, and kept only as long as needed. POPIA is designed in from the first line.

Always up during a promo

The game is a fast static build on scalable hosting, with a monitored backend for the leaderboard. It is built to hold up through a national campaign, and we fix same-day.

Prize integrity

Winner selection runs through a role-locked admin, every draw and board reset is logged, and only proven purchases qualify. The audit trail means a winner can always be stood behind.

05 · How we QA before anything ships

We prove it.
We do not
assert it.

Nothing reaches your shoppers on our word alone. Every build and every change clears the same gates before it goes live.

Prototype first

We build the core game feel in isolation and show it moving before committing to the engine, so the direction is agreed on proof, not a promise.

Two-round QA

Functional testing against the agreed scope, then pressure testing at the edges and under load, before anything is signed off.

Real devices

Tested on real phones, not just a desktop preview, because that is where it is played. Mobile-first, one-thumb.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor: readable contrast, clear focus, reduced-motion respected. It is a public promotion, so it has to work for everyone.

Watched motion

We watch the game actually run, at speed, and capture proof of it. Motion is verified, never assumed to work.

Independent sign-off

A fresh set of eyes runs the final QA pass, not the person who built it. You review a working version before it goes live.

06 · What we need from you to build it

A short list.
Then we start.

None of it has to be perfect on day one. The prototype week runs on very little; the rest can follow as the build progresses. What each brand supplies, that brand's marketing lead owns.

To start (the prototype and engine)

Chikka brand assets

Logo, colours, any characters or mascot, tone and copy guidelines, and sounds if you have them. From Bianca, the Chikka marketing lead. Rough is fine to begin; final polish comes at the branding phase.

A sign-off

An NDA (yours or ours) and a short Statement of Work agreeing the scope and price. Build starts the same week.

The game direction

Any must-haves or no-gos for the game feel. We prototype from there and agree the direction with you before the full build.

A campaign name and domain

What the campaign is called and any domain or subdomain preference. We can register one if you would rather.

For proof of purchase and prizes

No barcode to chase

Chikka is a deli product with no single barcode, and store scale codes change daily, so we do not try to read it off the slip. Anyone plays; only winners upload a slip to redeem. Nothing to track across thousands of store codes.

The slip QR decision

Whether IT can add a campaign QR to the till slip. If not yet, we launch on an in-store QR and add the slip QR later. Not a blocker either way.

Prize and cadence

What the prize is, how often it runs (weekly or monthly), and who handles physical fulfilment and redemption on your side.

Any brand or legal rules

Competition terms, SPAR brand guidelines, or anything legal or compliance wants reflected in how the promotion runs.

In short

Prototype week,
then live in weeks.

Sign off, a prototype week to agree the feel, then the engine and the Chikka game, so live in roughly six to eight weeks. Each brand after is a reskin, not a rebuild.
