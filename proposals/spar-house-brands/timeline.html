04 · How entry works, updated from the meeting

Play first.
Prove it
only to win.

Chikka Chicken is a deli product with no single barcode, so we do not try to read it off the slip. Instead, anyone can play, and only a winner is asked to upload their till slip to redeem. A "keep your till slip to redeem your prize" line sits at the bottom of the slip.

No barcode problem

Nothing depends on tracking thousands of changing store-level scale codes. Entry is by playing, so loose strips and two-piece portions qualify just as well as packaged.

Low friction, more entries

Asking for the slip only on a win removes the upload barrier that capped the Cerebral Salt campaign. More people play, only winners do the admin.

Minimal data

Name, contact number, and email, no demographic profiling. Winners are then contacted directly for a delivery address.

QR at point of sale, with IT

SPAR IT is checking whether a QR can be triggered at the till for Chikka, like the existing car competition. If not, the game still launches on in-store QRs.

05 · To start

Three things unlock the deposit and Phase 1.

A signed Statement of Work

An NDA and a short SOW confirming the scope, the milestones, and the price. This releases the deposit and starts the prototype.

Chikka brand assets

Logo, colours, characters, and tone from SPAR marketing, for the final game skins. Rough is fine to begin; polish comes at the branding phase.

The IT answer on the QR trigger

SPAR IT confirming whether a point-of-sale QR can be linked to Chikka. Not a blocker to start, the game launches on in-store QRs regardless.
