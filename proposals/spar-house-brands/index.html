01 · The Opportunity

One product today.
Every house brand
tomorrow.

Chicka Chicken is SPAR's in-house crumbed chicken, priced under KFC and Hungry Lion, already on the shelf in stores across the country. The product is not the problem. Getting a shopper to choose it, remember it, and come back for it is.

A game does that. It gives someone a reason to pick up the pack, keep the slip, and come back next week to beat their own score. Built right, the same engine then carries every house brand after Chicka, one reskin at a time.

The slip is litter

A till slip gets thrown away at the door. Turn it into a ticket: buy Chicka, scan the slip, play, and qualify to win.

Choice needs a hook

Affordable is not enough on its own. A high-score chase gives a shopper an actual reason to reach for Chicka over the name next to it.

It has to be rebrandable

One engine, reskinned per brand, not a fresh build every time. Chicka launches it; McCoy's and the laundry bar run on the same thing.

Proof of purchase, done fairly

Anyone can play. To claim a prize, a player proves the buy with their slip. The game is open, the reward is earned.

Browser, not an app

No App Store queue, no Apple or Google QA, same-day fixes, and no risk of a rebrand being flagged as a brand-new app.

One game live at a time

The engine hosts whichever brand is running, then hands the stage to the next. Simple to run, cheap to keep live.

05 · Technical Approach & Fairness

Built to reskin.
Fair to play.

The engine is browser-native and portrait-first, built on Phaser with an accessible DOM overlay. A single campaign layer, the brand name, palette, art, copy and links, reskins it per brand without touching the game logic. Proof of purchase validates a till slip before a score can qualify, and entrant data is kept minimal and POPIA-handled. Built AI-first with Claude Code and Lovable, so a multi-month studio timeline compresses into weeks without dropping quality. Included here as it matters for a public promotion: the architecture, and how fairness and data are handled.

Engine

Phaser + TypeScript

Portrait-first, one-thumb, canvas and WebGL, running in any modern browser with no app install.

Brand layer

One campaign file

Name, palette, art, copy and links swapped per brand in a single layer, with the game logic never touched.

Leaderboard

Hosted backend

A live high-score board, weekly or monthly prize windows, and one qualifying entry per validated slip.

Proof of purchase

Slip scan / upload

Validated against store, date, product and slip number before a score can qualify. No card data stored.

Entry

QR codes

An in-store QR from day one, and the slip-printed QR the moment SPAR IT can add it.

Compliance

POPIA by design

Minimal entrant data, consent and opt-out, encrypted contact details, and retention limits from day one.

One item to confirm on the slip

To validate a purchase we need four fields off the till slip: store, date and time, product line, and slip number. That is the short, specific list to take to SPAR IT about adding a campaign QR to the slip. The game launches on an in-store QR either way, and adds the slip QR the moment IT is ready.
