Cost & margin

Add purchase prices so margin reporting switches on.
Native ShopifyYour numbersAbout 20 minutes

Shopify has a built in "Cost per item" field. Once it is filled, you get a Profit by Product report and a stock export showing purchase price against sale price. No app, no extra cost.

  1. Go to Products, click Export, choose All products and Plain CSV.
  2. Open the file and find the column named exactly:
    Cost per item
  3. Fill in the purchase price for each product. Leave every other column untouched.
  4. Go to Products › Import, upload the file, tick Overwrite existing products with the same handle, and import.
  5. View margins at Analytics › Reports › Finances › Profit by product.
Template providedThe file Euroseat_Cost_Import_Template.csv shows the exact format. Delete its two example rows before importing. Full profit reports need the Shopify or Advanced plan; on Basic you still get the cost field and the stock export.

Payment methods

Activate iDEAL and your Germany and Belgium options.
Native ShopifyYour account detailsAbout 15 minutes

The layout is in place and shown in the walkthrough video. Only you can connect the payment accounts, because they verify against your business and banking details.

  1. Go to Settings › Payments.
  2. Activate your provider for iDEAL (Shopify Payments or Mollie) and complete verification with your business and bank details.
  3. Enable the standard methods you use for Germany and Belgium.
  4. Confirm Klarna is switched off, to match your current site.
  5. Place one test order to confirm checkout works end to end.
Only you can do thisPayment activation requires your own identity and banking verification. No agency can do it for you. The walkthrough video shows exactly where each detail goes.

4

Product automations

Four workflows in Shopify Flow. About ten minutes each.
Native · Shopify FlowPoint and clickAbout 30 minutes total

Install the free Shopify Flow app, then build these four. In Flow click Create workflow and set the trigger, condition and action as shown. Shopify's Sidekick can also draft each one if you paste the description.

4a  Remove the "new" tag automatically

  1. Trigger: Product created
  2. Action: Wait, set to 14 days
  3. Action: Remove product tags, tag:
    new

4b  Hide products when sold out

  1. Trigger: Inventory quantity changed
  2. Condition: total inventory is less than or equal to 0
  3. Action: Unpublish product

4c  Restore products when back in stock

  1. Trigger: Inventory quantity changed
  2. Condition: total inventory is greater than 0
  3. Action: Publish product

4d  Tag B2B orders with the company name

  1. Trigger: Order created
  2. Condition: order purchasing entity is a company
  3. Action: Add order tags, using this value so orders are searchable by company:
    {{ order.purchasingEntity.company.name }}
TipUse Turn on workflow after building each one, and test it with the preview tool before it goes live.

B2B, explained simply

What it is, what it is not, and how we set it up for you.
Native ShopifyHandled by ConstellationNo extra cost on your plan

Since 2 April 2026, Shopify includes B2B on every plan. It lets you sell to businesses with their own accounts and pricing, separate from your normal shop. Here is the honest picture so you know exactly what you have.

What it is (you have this)

Company accounts: group buyers under a company and see their orders together.
Up to 3 catalogues, each with its own price list for custom B2B pricing.
Native volume and quantity price breaks, which cover your lot pricing.
Payment terms such as Net 15, 30, 60 or 90 days.
Order search by company name, via the automation in step 4d.

What it is not (needs Plus, not required)

Unlimited catalogues. You have 3, assigned through Markets.
Assigning a catalogue directly to one specific company.
Deposits and partial payments at checkout.
How we use itConstellation builds your B2B setup directly through the Shopify API once you grant access. All you provide is a list: which customers are companies, and the pricing each should see. We configure the companies, catalogues and price lists; you simply invite your buyers. For a furniture reseller, 3 catalogues is almost always enough.
