The model

I invest the work, not a bill.

Rather than charging Conexxus this amount as a fee, I contribute it as my investment into the business and take equity in exchange. I succeed only when Conexxus does.

The equity is earned as the work lands: a share is recognised as each of the three builds is accepted against its agreed definition of done. The specific terms we agree together and document properly, with brief legal and tax sign-off, so the partnership is real and protected from day one. If the role continues beyond this first phase, ongoing contribution earns ongoing equity in the normal way.

The role

Fractional Head of Design & AI Implementation.

I propose joining around two focused days a week. Fractional is deliberate: you get senior design and AI leadership without the cost of a full-time executive hire, at the stage when that leadership matters most. Beyond the three builds, the role means owning the design system, the AI roadmap, and the community engine as Conexxus grows.
