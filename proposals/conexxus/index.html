Conexxus has built something real: South Africa's expert network, where people get paid, trustworthy advice from verified professionals over email, voice notes, or video. The first professionals are on, the first consultations are done, and the satisfaction is there.

05 · How We Work Together

A real partnership. Documented properly.

None of this is complicated to set up. It simply makes the partnership real and protects both sides from day one.

Invest, not bill

I contribute the work as my investment into Conexxus and take equity in exchange, rather than charging a fee. I do well when you do well.

Earned on delivery

Equity is recognised as each of the three builds is accepted against its agreed definition of done, held in escrow until then. Ownership always matches value delivered.

Documented and protected

A shareholders and subscription agreement, written assignment of all delivered work to Conexxus, a vesting schedule, and standard leaver terms.

Tax and legal sign-off

Issuing shares for work has tax consequences in South Africa, so both sides take brief professional advice before signing.

The role

Fractional Head of Design and AI Implementation, around two focused days a week. Senior leadership without a full-time hire.

Delivery

The work is delivered through my studio, Constellation, under a service agreement, with the trust as the shareholder. A clean structure, clearly recorded.
