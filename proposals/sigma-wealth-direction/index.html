Phase 0 · Positioning

A modern presence for an established trust.

The advisor your parents trusted, now built for the people who'll inherit that trust.

Sigma is not repositioning away from its roots, a family-practice, FSCA-licensed advisory based in Bethlehem, Free State. What's changing is how that trust reads to a younger audience raised on modern, transparent, self-serve digital experiences. This is not a brand from scratch: it is your real navy and sage, your real bilingual commitment, your real team, carried by a digital presence that finally matches the quality of the advice.

Phase 2 · References we physically studied

What we take from each, and how it's built

Before designing anything, we looked at your current site and four real South African wealth-advisory competitors, including theonefs.co.za (your own named closest competitor), plus one international wealth-management site for craft and polish.

Starting point
